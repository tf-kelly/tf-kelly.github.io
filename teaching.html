I have taught an average of twelve hours per week during university term since 2023, across a range of Oxford and Cambridge colleges. I am currently a Stipendiary Lecturer in Classical Languages at Magdalen College, Oxford, and supervise for several Cambridge colleges alongside my fellowship at Darwin.

Supervision and tutorial teaching

Greek and Latin language supervisions at all levels, including the Linguistic Description of Greek and Latin for advanced undergraduates at Magdalen; Greek literature at all levels; Part IB Ancient Medicine.

Colleges: Magdalen, University, St Hilda’s, New, and Exeter (Oxford); Pembroke, Gonville and Caius, Queens’, Trinity, and Homerton (Cambridge).

Class teaching

Faculty of Classics, University of Oxford (2023–): Elementary Greek and Latin (Mods); Graduate Latin; set-text reading classes for undergraduates and graduates.

Faculty of Classics, University of Cambridge (2019–20): set-text reading classes and class language teaching for undergraduates and MPhil students.

Summer schools

JACT Greek Summer School (2019, 2022–): 30-hour intensive Greek course for sixth-formers. Directed a production of Sophocles’ Ajax in Greek at the end of the 2023 course.

Cambridge Greek Summer School Online (2020): intensive beginner Greek for incoming Part IA undergraduates.

Resources

Designed a new set of Latin resources for the revamped Mods Latin language course at Oxford, adopted by the Faculty and available to all tutors and students through Canvas (2024).

Examining and admissions

Examiner, Undergraduate Final Examinations, Faculty of Classics, Oxford (2023, 2026); marker, Classics Aptitude Test (2024). Undergraduate admissions interviewer: St Hilda’s, Oxford (2024); Faculty of Classics, Oxford (2023); Girton College, Cambridge (2019).
