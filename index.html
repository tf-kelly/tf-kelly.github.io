I am the Mary and Moses Finley Research Fellow at Darwin College, Cambridge, a Visiting Researcher on The Agrimensores and Roman Mathematics (Newcastle), and College Lecturer in Classical Languages at Magdalen College, Oxford. Most of my work is on ancient scientific and technical writing, particularly ancient mathematics, and on linguistic variation in ancient Greek and Latin. I am especially interested in the conditions of text production in antiquity — other interests include phonology and orthography in Roman-period documentary papyri, dictation in Cicero’s correspondence, the Roman agrimensores, Greek and Latin composition, and football refereeing.
