In addition to my fellowship at Darwin, I am a Visiting Researcher on The Agrimensores and Roman Mathematics (Newcastle), where I lead the linguistic study accompanying the project's edition of the Corpus Agrimensorum Romanorum, and College Lecturer in Classical Languages at Magdalen College, Oxford. Most of my work is on linguistic variation in ancient Greek and Latin and the conditions of text production in antiquity. Other research interests include phonology and orthography in Roman-period documentary papyri, dictation and writing in Cicero's correspondence, the Roman agrimensores, ancient practical mathematics, Greek and Latin composition, and football refereeing.

Recent and forthcoming work: ‘Diagrams in Ancient Greek Practical Mathematics’ (forthcoming, Historia Mathematica); ‘Ptolemy’s Geographical Guide and the Linguistic Apparatus of Ancient Greek Mathematics’ (under review, Isis). Two further articles are in preparation: on dictation in Cicero’s letters to Atticus, and on phonological and orthographic variation in Roman-period documentary papyri.
