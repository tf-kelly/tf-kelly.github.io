I am currently Moses and Mary Finley Research Fellow in Ancient History at Darwin College, Cambridge, as well as a visiting researcher on the project The Agrimensores and Roman Mathematics (Newcastle), and College Lecturer in Classical Languages at Magdalen College, Oxford. Most of my work is on ancient scientific and technical writing, particularly ancient mathematics, but I am also interested in linguistic variation in ancient Greek and Latin and the conditions of text production in antiquity.
